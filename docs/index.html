Pro Tips for Best Results

Click each card to discover expert techniques

📸

Image Quality

Click to learn more

Use High-Resolution Images

For optimal results, use images with at least 1920x1080 resolution. Higher resolution provides more detail for the AI to work with, resulting in cleaner edges and better subject separation.

🎯

Clean Backgrounds

Click to learn more

Minimize Background Noise

Images with less cluttered backgrounds process better. If possible, choose photos with clear subject-background separation. Plain or contrasting backgrounds yield the most precise results.

💡

Lighting Matters

Click to learn more

Good Lighting = Better Results

Well-lit images with even lighting across the subject produce superior results. Avoid heavy shadows or extreme backlighting, as these can confuse edge detection.

🔍

Subject Focus

Click to learn more

Sharp Focus on Subject

Ensure your main subject is in sharp focus. Blurry or out-of-focus images make it harder for the AI to distinguish edges accurately, especially with fine details like hair.

🎨

Color Contrast

Click to learn more

Strong Subject-Background Contrast

Images where the subject's colors differ significantly from the background are easier to process. Avoid subjects wearing colors that blend with the background.

⚡

File Formats

Click to learn more

Choose Quality Formats

Use PNG or high-quality JPG formats for input. Heavily compressed images may have artifacts that affect edge precision. The output is always saved as high-quality transparent PNG.
